# Kanvi logo v2

**Approved direction:** Dynamisk overlap + `kanvi?`

The three overlapping forms remain the primary symbol. The full wordmark explicitly reads **kanvi?**. The question mark is green with a yellow accent/dot.

## Assets

- `kanvi-logo.svg` — primary logo on light backgrounds
- `kanvi-logo-dark.svg` — full logo on dark backgrounds
- `kanvi-mark.svg` — Dynamisk overlap symbol
- `kanvi-wordmark.svg` — wordmark
- `kanvi-app-icon.svg` — app/icon treatment

## Usage

Use the supplied SVG assets directly. Do not recreate the logo from screenshots or with CSS. Use the full logo in navigation and marketing contexts and the mark alone for favicon, app icon and compact contexts.

Do not add a calendar, shadows or outlines, distort proportions, or arbitrarily recolor the three forms.

## Colors

- Navy `#0B2540`
- Green `#16A34A`
- Mint `#86EFAC`
- Yellow `#FBBF24`
- Cream `#FFFDF8`

## Codex

Use `kanvi-logo.svg` by default on light surfaces and `kanvi-logo-dark.svg` on dark surfaces. Use `kanvi-mark.svg` for compact icon contexts. Preserve the question mark in the full brand presentation.
